8-Inch L-Shape Digital Menu Board | Quad-Core WiFi Tablet

The 8-inch L-shape digital menu board is a small, high-performance Android tablet. It is made for professional and self-service use. It delivers smooth performance by quad-core digital signage for restaurant menu displays, POS systems, and smart retail terminals.

This digital menu board runs on Android 11. It supports 802.11b/g/n Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 4.0, and Gigabit Ethernet. This ensures fast and stable connectivity. The 800×1280 IPS display provides clear visuals and bright colors. The front 2.0MP camera allows for real-time interaction and remote monitoring.

Designed for reliability and easy integration, it has DC power input, USB, and Serial ports. This makes it simple for software developers and OEM/ODM partners to deploy and customize systems.

With its sleek L-shaped design, stable performance, and strong connectivity. This digital menu board is the perfect hardware platform for interactive kiosks, smart displays, and enterprise automation projects worldwide.
